Chapter II

Forge · Scaling · Soul

Every armament in the Lands Between carries with it a story. Some were forged by the astrologers of old, their blades tempered in moonlight. Others were quenched in the blood of gods — and drink still of any unwary wielder.

The Smithing Hewg, chained deep within the Roundtable Hold, can raise a common longsword to legend — if fed enough Smithing Stones. But to awaken a weapon's true affinity, one must turn to Ashes of War: memories of battles past, granted form and affixed to one's chosen blade.

A Heavy affinity rewards the stalwart knight, the barbarian warlord. Keen favors the lithe duelist. Magic sings to the Carian scholar, Fire roars for the Fire Monk, and Holy shines for those still faithful to the Golden Order. There are whispers too, of Occult and Bleed and Poison — affinities the Order would rather see buried.

Choose well, Tarnished. A blade does not merely cut. It shapes the soul of its wielder.
